# Still-frame review — run on every round

Capture key beats + random frames (`capture.cjs … random 8`), make a contact sheet, LOOK at each one, then write
a short verdict: what fails, the single biggest fix, done. Fix, recapture, repeat. Rounds by budget: quick 1,
standard 3, max until every box passes.

## Each still, as if it were a frame from a finished film
1. **Reads at thumbnail size?** Hero silhouette clear, one focal point, nothing important cut by the frame edge.
2. **Genre/style is unmistakable?** Would someone call it anime / ink-wash / papercut … at a glance? Or does it
   read as "web illustration", "clip art", "3D", "vector corporate"?
3. **Forms** — no pill/blob shapes where the design needs planes; no repeated identical trees/props; no empty
   unfinished areas; scale reads (occlusion, haze, tiny reference object).
4. **Light** — one direction, coloured shadows, glows tint their surroundings, no grey mud.
5. **Line** — weight varies, shadow side heavier, open lines taper, no hairline outlines on big shapes.
6. **Texture** — paper/gouache/grain present; flat fills only where the style wants them.
7. **Text** (if any) legible, inside safe area (Shorts: keep y 300–1500 of 1920 clear of UI).

## Across frames (contact sheet / scrub the MP4)
8. Timing: holds exist, moves have anticipation and settle, nothing floats at constant speed.
9. Continuity: design stays on model, light direction constant, no popping layers or seams in tiling mist.
10. Story beats land in order at the times in the shot list; climax is the biggest frame.
11. Ending resolves (hold + fade), loop point clean if looping.

## Sound (measure — you cannot listen)
12. Every visual event has a cue within ±1 frame; music swells into the climax.
13. Levels per second rise toward the climax (print RMS per second); integrated -14 LUFS (±0.5), true peak ≈ -1 dB or lower.
14. No clicks: max sample-to-sample jump < 0.3 outside impacts.

Report honestly to the user which boxes still fail.
